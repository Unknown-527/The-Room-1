Darkness lurks, shadows escape. People return, silhouettes appear. Familiar most, unfamiliar more. The unfamiliar yet familiar in a way.

People sleep but one remains deep awake, afraid. The unfamiliar is frighteningly familiar to him, the familiar isn't. He stays in his bed, a fear in his head, but hope in his hands.

He is destined to die but has to face another night, yet another night.
